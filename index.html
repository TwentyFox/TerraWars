How to Play

  • Use numbers 1-6 to start building units.
  • Use your mouse to position this unit, and left click to construct it. Cancel the action with right click.
  • Click on units to make them stop/go. Use this to tactically position your units, and coordinate your attacks.
  • Construct miners to mine ore to collect more money.
  • Navigate the battlefield with the Up/Down arrow keys.

Tips

  • The enemy base is defended by 2 gunships. These units are powerful, so save your money/firepower until you can overcome their autocannons.
  • The enemy will constantly bombard you with units. Always be prepared for an attack.
  • Build miners early on to build your economy. Remember, more money means more units.
